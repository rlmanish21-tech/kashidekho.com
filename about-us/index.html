About Kashi Dekho

More Than a Tour.
A Local Way to Experience Kashi.

Kashi is not simply a destination to visit. It is a city of living traditions, sacred rituals, timeless streets, vibrant food and stories that reveal themselves slowly. Kashi Dekho creates thoughtfully planned journeys to help travellers experience that side of Varanasi.

KASHI
Our Story

Discover Kashi Beyond Sightseeing

Kashi Dekho is built around a simple idea: a visit to Varanasi should feel like an experience, not a checklist.

Our journeys bring together the spiritual landmarks of Kashi with the atmosphere of its ghats, old lanes, food, heritage and local culture. From a short Kashi Yatra to a longer journey through Ayodhya, Prayagraj or Vindhyachal, we aim to make planning simple and the experience memorable.

Meet the Founder
Founder, Kashi Dekho

Manish Mishra

Kashi Dekho is founded by Manish Mishra with the vision of creating a more thoughtful way for travellers to experience Varanasi and its surrounding destinations.

The brand focuses on curated journeys, clear planning and experiences that connect travellers with the spiritual and cultural character of Kashi.
